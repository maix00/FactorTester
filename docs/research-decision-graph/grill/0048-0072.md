# Grill Record: Questions 48–72

The entries preserve the actual question branch, user correction, final
meaning, and acceptance consequence. Quotation marks around a user response
denote the durable response text; question 48 is recovered from the compacted
task record rather than a verbatim prompt.

Shared code, document, statistical sources, and per-question acceptance
scenarios are indexed in [the evidence registry](evidence-registry.md).

## 48 — Mandatory backend completion

**Question/proposal.** Does “unconditional completion” mean any requested
operator is implemented immediately? Recommended: an admissible missing
general capability becomes mandatory platform work, but does not bypass
approval, testing, release, or scope control.

**User response.** Accepted in the compacted record.

**Resolution.** Pause only dependent research branches. Continue unaffected
jobs and paths. A capability receipt, not an Agent assertion, resumes work.

**Rejected case.** A factor-specific or semantically approximate primitive is
not accepted merely to unblock one run.

**Evidence/acceptance.** Server Maintenance boundary; test branch-local pause,
continued unrelated jobs, approval, conformance, release, and receipt resume.

## 49 — Result-before and result-after enrichment

**Question/proposal.** May an Agent propose conditional enrichment before or
after seeing results? Recommended: preregister mechanism-based enrichment
before results; after IC sign change, non-monotonic groups, tail instability,
or cost erosion, create a new hypothesis/trial and do not reuse an exposed
holdout.

**User response.** “接受”.

**Resolution.** Results can inspire the next experiment but cannot retroactively
rescue the current one.

**Evidence/acceptance.** Trial and holdout lineage must show a new hypothesis
version and an unconsumed confirmation sample.

## 50 — Promoting experience to a Draft path

**Question/proposal.** When can Provisional Memory propose a Draft Graph path?
Recommended: one qualified case may propose when a documented statistical,
market, or industry rule supports it; otherwise require configurable
independent repetition with failures and counterexamples retained.

**User response.** “可以”.

**Resolution.** Neither channel activates directly. Replay, like-for-like
shadow comparison, and high-risk audit remain mandatory.

**Rejected case.** One successful, unsupported experiment does not become an
edge.

## 51 — Bounded conditioning search

**Question/proposal.** Should the Agent try every matching conditioning method?
Recommended: return a small mechanism-ranked set, record each transformation,
auxiliary, threshold, scale, and combination in the trial ledger, then use
incremental addition and ablation.

**User response.** “可以”.

**Resolution.** Candidate count is budgeted; excessive candidates are ranked or
the hypothesis is narrowed.

**Evidence/acceptance.** No unrecorded combinatorial sweep; retain candidate
selection rationale and ablation evidence.

## 52 — Does an auxiliary need standalone alpha?

**Question/proposal.** Recommended: not always. Preserve a main-only baseline;
evaluate state/confidence/risk/liquidity auxiliaries by interaction,
incremental value, coverage, stability, and cost. Use aux-only only when its
semantics make standalone trading meaningful.

**User response.** Accepted, then asked whether candidates could come from
previous or other-Agent backtests and specifically cited local SgCCS.

**Resolution.** A stable independently predictive auxiliary, or selection of
its direction/weight, changes the research class to multi-factor. Candidate
provenance and reuse scope were continued in question 53.

## 53 — Candidate evidence tiers

**Question/proposal.** Recommended four tiers: reusable in-scope immutable
evidence; prior evidence requiring transfer validation; unconfirmed library
factor; Agent-created factor. SgCCS is discoverable but reusable only when hash,
evidence, and scope match.

**User response.** “可以”.

**Resolution.** Main/auxiliary is an experiment role, not a permanent factor
label. Confirmed factors are prioritized but do not exclude untested or new
candidates.

**Acceptance.** Search results expose tier and scope, not a binary valid flag.

## 54 — Scope evidence and `$Rev`

**Question/proposal.** The initial proposal bound scope confirmation to immutable
factor/AST, data, RunSpec, validation, product, frequency, period, state,
holding period, strategy, cost, and accounting evidence, but incorrectly
described `$Rev` as a research-result label.

**User response.** Challenged the `$Rev` meaning and warned against both
restricting Agents to known factors and ignoring market background.

**Resolution.** Code inspection showed `$Rev` is a concrete configuration
direction-negation parameter. It is neither evidence status, an independent
factor, nor proof of reversal. Scope evidence remains structured and
multi-valued.

**Supersedes.** The `$Rev` sentence in the original proposal is rejected.

## 55 — Hard compatibility versus soft evidence

**Question/proposal.** Recommended hard exclusions only for permission, actual
data absence, invalid timing, or incompatible product semantics. Historical
scope, market state, other-Agent evidence, related mechanisms, and
counterexamples are soft ranking evidence.

**User response.** Accepted with the requirement to explicitly warn the Agent
not to inspect only those factors.

**Resolution.** Market context must be considered but never forms a candidate
whitelist.

## 56 — Mandatory non-whitelist prompt

**Question/proposal.** Add a non-optional prompt stating that confirmed/highly
related factors are starting points, and an exit field saying whether expanded
search was performed or why it was skipped.

**User response.** “可以”.

**Resolution.** Expanded search uses compact queries rather than full-library
context and can include out-of-scope, untested, other-Agent, and newly
constructed candidates.

**Acceptance.** Candidate discovery cannot exit without the search-or-reason
evidence field.

## 57 — Open-ended market states

**Question/proposal.** Known volatility, trend, liquidity, term-structure, and
seasonal states are starting points, not a closed taxonomy. A new state needs a
computable point-in-time definition.

**User response.** “接受”.

**Resolution.** A state proposed after outcome inspection enters the next
trial. An unmatched period is “unclassified,” not “no market context.”

## 58 — Evidence reuse versus rerun

**Question/proposal.** Reuse only when factor/AST, data snapshot, RunSpec,
product configuration, cost convention, and backend receipt match. Scope
differences support a transfer hypothesis but require current-scope
validation. Name-only evidence is a clue.

**User response.** “接受”.

**Resolution.** Backend/accounting semantic changes trigger affected replay.
Model or Agent identity changes alone do not.

## 59 — Who generalizes experience?

**Initial question/proposal.** Do not wake an Agent after every run. Write
compact Provisional Memory deterministically and trigger review only for new
general mechanisms/gaps, repetition threshold, counterevidence, reusable scope
evidence, or changed norms.

**User response.** “这个是服务器agent的工作吧？”

**Revised question.** Assign generalization to deterministic server triggers
plus an on-demand Server Maintenance Agent; client Research Agents only
produce evidence and candidate suggestions.

**User response.** “接受”.

**Resolution.** Graph evolution is not a client research-node side effect and
does not spawn a reviewer for every backtest.

## 60 — Graph-version migration

**Question/proposal.** Pin a branch to its starting graph version; let already
running jobs finish; pause only branches materially affected by a new graph;
switch at a safe checkpoint with a migration receipt; start new branches on
the latest active graph.

**User response.** “接受”.

**Resolution.** New policy may reassess old evidence but cannot rewrite its
historical graph identity or decision.

## 61 — Composition versus primitive proliferation

**Question/proposal.** An exact canonical composition can satisfy a missing
capability if it is serializable, auditable, incremental where required, and
semantically identical; otherwise add a first-class operator or strategy
capability.

**User response.** Accepted with two objections: single-factor tests must not
silently become multi-factor combinations, and enrichment may revise an
existing family or accept a `FactorParam` rather than always create a new
family.

**Resolution.** Capability completion concerns exact research ability, not
primitive count. Factor class and versioning were refined in questions 62–66.

## 62 — Existing-family enrichment and immutability

**Question/proposal.** Permit an existing family to add `FactorParam` and
produce distinct configurations; never overwrite tested formulas. Semantic
repair creates a new family version and marks the affected old scope.

**User response.** “可以”.

**Resolution.** Each FactorParam binding/configuration is an independent
immutable trial. Timing, alignment, missingness, and product scope require
explicit checks.

## 63 — Proposed aligned/raw dual mode

**Question/proposal.** The initial proposal offered `aligned_signal` for
reusing a frozen auxiliary signal and `raw_expression` for using its inner
expression, with FactorRef identity, cycle prevention, and DAG limits.

**User response.** Questioned why raw expression should not be preferred for
precomputed and live paths and shared computation, while noting that prior
auxiliary validation might no longer transfer.

**Disposition.** Superseded by question 65. The concern about evidence transfer
is retained; the two-mode FactorParam API is not.

## 64 — Proposed two-layer shared DAG

**Question/proposal.** The proposal separated raw calculation and aligned
signal nodes and exposed both choices through FactorParam.

**User response.** Rejected this as too complex and assigned precomputed/live
choice to backtest or real-trading execution rather than factor expression.
Preferred raw expression inside FactorExpr.

**Disposition.** Superseded by question 65. Shared computation remains an
execution-engine responsibility.

## 65 — Final FactorParam boundary

**Question/proposal.** `FactorParam = raw expression composition`;
`FactorFamily = one final signal alignment`; backtest/trading owns
multi-frequency conditioning; execution owns shared DAG, cache, and
precomputed/live choice.

**User response.** “接受”.

**Resolution.** A parent construction is revalidated as a new factor; child
evidence is a prior, not automatically inherited proof.

## 66 — Factor evidence versus strategy evidence

**Question/proposal.** Raw auxiliary composition creates a new factor version.
An auxiliary controlling entry, holding, exit, position, or turnover creates
factor-plus-strategy evidence and cannot claim the unchanged factor improved.

**User response.** “接受”.

**Acceptance.** Persist factor hash, strategy-policy hash, auxiliary hash, and
evidence class; never disguise a strategy rule as FactorExpr.

## 67 — Initial operator scope

**Question/proposal.** Implement the explicitly needed `tanh`, expose an
existing `where` kernel through a public typed interface, and verify raw
FactorParam nesting/version/timing/incremental behavior. Discover future
operators dynamically instead of building a fixed operator zoo.

**User response.** Accepted and required careful operator folder hierarchy.

**Impact.** Triggered questions 68–72 and architecture inspection.

## 68 — Initial directory proposal

**Question/proposal.** Initially proposed staged migration into an
`expr/operators/` hierarchy, compatibility re-exports, later duplicate
`WhereOp` removal/registry, then `tanh` and public `where`.

**User response.** Accepted conditionally: existing operators must remain
unbroken.

**Disposition.** Directory shape revised by question 72; staged,
behavior-preserving migration and compatibility requirement remain.

## 69 — Migration contract snapshot

**Question/proposal.** Freeze imports, operator catalog, structural hashes,
aliases/LaTeX, FactorParam resolution, batch numerical/NaN/broadcast behavior,
incremental support/parity, source/tree checks, generated SDK Pyright, and
representative factor/SgCCS smoke behavior before movement.

**User response.** Accepted and directed the grill back toward Agent flow.

**Resolution.** Pure migration permits zero intentional semantic change. Found
bugs become separate batches.

## 70 — Planning versus Research autonomy

**Question/proposal.** Planning sees the whole workspace and obtains user
confirmation for primary scope. Research may find/create auxiliaries, modify
factors, use raw FactorParam, and route strategy or multi-factor semantics
without repeated approval inside that scope.

**User response.** “接受”.

**Resolution.** A material change in primary research objective returns to
Planning/user; auxiliary experiments do not.

## 71 — Discoverability of experimental factors

**Question/proposal.** Register a new/modified factor locally as
`experimental`; after a run, publish source-free server metadata, role, and job
reference. Preserve failed/contradicted status; source sharing remains
permission-controlled.

**User response.** “可以”.

**Resolution.** Directory discoverability does not imply validation or source
upload.

## 72 — Evidence-driven operator architecture correction

**Question/proposal.** Architecture inspection found `expr/operators/` to be a
shallow Module with no added Depth or Leverage. Recommended the existing flat
semantic modules, a sole `WhereOp` in `conditional.py`, shared pointwise
batch/incremental math, separate authoring catalog, and no one-operator files.

**User response.** “接受”.

**Resolution.** This explicitly revises question 68. Strategy conditioning
stays outside FactorExpr and the Author SDK keeps its explicit allowlist.

**Evidence/acceptance.** Existing module layout, duplicate `WhereOp`, SDK
allowlist, expression contracts, and question 69 migration gates.
